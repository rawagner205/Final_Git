How to Play:
Navigate the player robot left and right by using either A and D keys or left and right arrow keys. In the environment there will be various objects to interact with. Static signs cannot be collected, but other objects can, and they will be stored in the player inventory. To use these items, use V to select a slot, C to use an item, or X to drop it. Each item has its own unique ability, whether that is a useful message, the key to passing a certain obstacle, or allowing the player to move in a new way. Use this inventory’s limited space wisely to choose the correct items to navigate the challenges posed by each level to reach its end.

Step-by-step walkthrough of intro level:
1. Walk to the right to pick up the first item and view the message on the sign.
2. Walk left to pick up the item near the edge of the ground platform. Walk to the triangle and it will be destroyed on contact. 
3. Walk right and pick up the item before the platform. Use V to navigate to “JumpTool” in the inventory and then use C to activate it.
4. Press the spacebar to jump over the platform. 
5. Walk right until you come to the next item. Use X to discard any of the items in your inventory to pick up this new one.
6. Walk to the door at the end of the level. It will be destroyed on contact, just like the triangle. Walk through the gap it leaves to complete the level. 

Step-by-step walkthrough of level 1:
1. Walk to the right to pick up the first item. Press 'C' to activate it for the ability to jump.
2. Jump on the platforms until you reach the purple platform. Collect and activate the item on this platform to gain extra jump height. 
3. Turn around and jump up the platforms until you reach the top one. Collect the key item there. 
4. Continue going right along the platforms. When the next platforms above appear, jump up those until you reach the top. Use 'X' to drop an item (not the key) from your inventory to pick up the item on the top platform. Navigate to "RemoteControl" in the inventory and activate this item. A large block will drop from the sky as a result.
5. Walk across this block to get to the yellow trapdoor in the ground. It will be destroyed on contact. Walk into the hole it leaves to complete the level. 

Step-by-step walkthrough of level 2:
1. Walk to the right and pick up the item there. Press 'C' to activate the item - this will reverse the gravity of the player robot. 
2. Walk to the right and allow the player to fall upwards to a yellow block. Walk to the left and press 'X' to drop the AntigravBooster item - make sure that this item drops somewhere it can be re-collected. 
3. Re-collect the AntigravBooster. Drop down into the hole and pick up the Hammer item. 
4. Activate the AntigravBooster again to return to the yellow block, which should be destroyed on contact. 
5. When the player hits the ceiling, walk to the left and drop down again. Pick up the yellow key. 
6. Drop down the shaft at the end of the hall. Walk towards the yellow rectangle set into the wall. It will be destroyed on contact. Walk through the gap it leaves to complete the game. 
7. Press 'Play Again' to restart the game.
