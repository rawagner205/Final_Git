How to Play:
Navigate the player robot backward and forward by using either A and D keys or left and right arrow keys. In the environment there will be various objects to interact with. Static signs cannot be collected, but other objects can, and they will be stored in the player inventory. To use these items, use V to select a slot, C to use an item, or X to drop it. Each item has its own unique ability, whether that is a useful message, the key to passing a certain obstacle, or allowing the player to move in a new way. Use this inventory’s limited space wisely to choose the correct items to navigate the challenges posed by the level to reach its end.

Step-by-step walkthrough:
1. Walk forward to pick up the first item and view the message on the sign.
2. Walk backwards to pick up the item near the back wall. Walk to the triangle and it will be destroyed on contact. 
3. Walk forward and pick up the item before the platform. Use V to navigate to “JumpTool” in the inventory and then use C to activate it.
4. Tap the spacebar to jump over the platform. 
5. Walk forward until you come to the next item. Use X to discard any of the items in your inventory to pick up this new one.
6. Walk to the door at the end of the level. It will be destroyed on contact, just like the triangle. Walk through the gap it leaves and the level will end.
